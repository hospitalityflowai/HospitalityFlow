# Future Ideas

Field evidence from first real hotel shift — 4 August 2026.  
These items capture product direction confirmed by live operations (not immediate defects).

---

## Think like an experienced Hotel Duty Manager

Date: 2026-08-04  
Category: Product philosophy  
Description: Hospitality Flow should reason like an experienced Hotel Duty Manager — deciding what the next shift needs — rather than simply rewriting source text.  
Potential value: Higher trust and faster operational handover decisions.  
Notes: Reinforced by real-shift testing; aligns with field-testing AI Design Principles.

---

## Hard rule: keep guest, room, request, payment and action linked

Date: 2026-08-04  
Category: Context integrity  
Description: Guest names, room numbers, requests, payments and actions must always remain connected as one operational unit across every section.  
Potential value: Prevents orphaned fragments that cannot be actioned safely.  
Notes: Multiple bugs from this shift are symptoms of this rule failing; treat as a standing product constraint.

---

## Accuracy over AI creativity

Date: 2026-08-04  
Category: AI quality bar  
Description: Operational accuracy is more important than AI creativity. Prefer returning less information when that information is correct.  
Potential value: Fewer operational mistakes and higher staff confidence.  
Notes: Apply especially to Briefing, VIP, Finance, Reception and Shift Intelligence.

---

## Validate every future change against field-testing handovers

Date: 2026-08-04  
Category: Quality process  
Description: Every future improvement should be checked against real hotel handovers collected in field testing before it is considered complete.  
Potential value: Stops regressions that only appear with messy live notes.  
Notes: Use Scenario 001 and later scenarios as the acceptance baseline.
