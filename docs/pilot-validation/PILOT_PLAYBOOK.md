# Hospitality Flow Pilot Playbook

## Purpose

Pilot sessions exist to learn.

They are not sales demonstrations.

The objective is to observe real operational behaviour.

---

## Before the Session

Confirm:

- Hotel prepared
- Demo working
- Hotel profile configured
- Example handover ready
- Internet connection
- Notebook available
- Validation template copied
- Know the participant's role

---

## Session Structure

### 1. Introduction

2 minutes.

Explain the operational problem Hospitality Flow solves.

Do not explain every feature.

### 2. Observation

Allow the participant to explore.

Avoid interrupting.

Avoid teaching unless they become completely stuck.

### 3. Questions

Ask open questions.

Examples:

- "What would you do first?"
- "What feels useful?"
- "What feels unnecessary?"
- "What information would you still look for elsewhere?"

### 4. Validation

Ask about:

- Today's Briefing
- Timeline
- Hotel Status
- Recommendations
- Overall trust

### 5. Closing

Thank them.

Explain next steps.

Ask permission for future follow-up.

---

## Behaviours to Observe

- Did they read Today's Briefing first?
- Did they notice Timeline?
- Did they understand Hotel Status?
- Did they scroll?
- Did they hesitate?
- Did they look confused?
- Did they ignore sections?
- Did they verify information elsewhere?
- Did they smile or react positively?
- Did they naturally understand the interface?

---

## Things NOT to Do

- Do not explain immediately.
- Do not defend the product.
- Do not argue.
- Do not ask leading questions.
- Do not finish their sentences.
- Do not ignore criticism.
- Do not promise features.

---

## Questions to Ask

- If Hospitality Flow disappeared tomorrow, what would you miss?
- What would stop you using it?
- Which screen was most valuable?
- Which screen added the least value?
- What surprised you?
- Did anything reduce your trust?
- Would this save your team time?
- Would you use it every shift?

---

## After the Session

- Complete the validation template.
- Transfer improvements to UX Backlog.
- Transfer observations to Customer Insights.
- Record important quotations.
- Record your own observations separately from participant feedback.

---

## Success Metrics

- Participant understood product without explanation.
- Participant trusted the briefing.
- Participant used Timeline.
- Participant understood priorities.
- Participant estimated meaningful time savings.
- Participant would continue using HF.
- Participant would recommend HF.

---

## Rule

Never optimise Hospitality Flow based on a single opinion.

Repeated evidence wins.

---

Review after every pilot.
